The Binding Clauses

Terms & Conditions

Section I
Asks of Shreyas Shilimkar
01
🚫No Cutting of Convo
Chinmayee commits to sharing complete thoughts without abruptly stopping mid-conversation. When she has concerns about Shreyas's behaviour while speaking, she shall voice them immediately rather than withdrawing with deflective words.
02
🗣️Say Out What You Need
Chinmayee shall articulate her needs and wishes directly rather than expecting Shreyas to understand them by implication. She is empowered to ask for any behaviour or action she desires from him.
03
💛Self-Blame
Chinmayee agrees to release the habit of accepting blame for every difficulty. Where fault lies with Shreyas, she must name it openly — even if it leads to a momentary conflict. Her truth matters.
04
🤝IRL Movement
Both parties acknowledge that physical comfort is a shared responsibility — "टाळ्या एका हाताने वाजत नाही". Chinmayee is encouraged to ease into physical comfort during meetings — body language, posture, and presence all count.
05
🔚Not the Same Ending
Should either party choose to exit this Bond, the departure must be clear, honest, and final — delivered with enough sincerity that it closes the door completely. No ambiguity. No lingering. A clean and compassionate end.
Section II
Asks of Chinmayee Holay
01
🫂Random Hugs
Shreyas acknowledges his earlier hesitation and now fully commits to offering spontaneous, warm hugs — stepping past self-doubt and into presence whenever the moment calls for it.
02
🤲Holding Hands
Shreyas, having previously held himself back through feelings of unworthiness, now agrees to this act of closeness — freely and without reservation.
03
✨Brushing Through Hair
As per the testimony of Chinmayee Holay, this tender gesture — apparently adored by her — is now officially included in Shreyas's agreed acts of care and affection. A simple thing that means the world.
04
🌇Meet-ups & Dates
Both parties shall invest in quality time together — naturally, without performance. Both are expected to initiate meetings. Frequency shall be balanced, respecting that each person has a life, goals, and space of their own.
05
🧠Not Overthinking
Shreyas commits to releasing the habit of misreading Chinmayee's body language as discomfort. What he saw as tension was simply her ease. He shall give her the benefit of the doubt, always.
06
👁️Mulin Sharkha Lazzna — Hold the Gaze
Shreyas agrees to hold eye contact when Chinmayee offers it — no longer looking away. To look away when someone reaches for your eyes is to reject them silently. That stops now.
